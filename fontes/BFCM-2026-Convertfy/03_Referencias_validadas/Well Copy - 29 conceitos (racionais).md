**1. Store Credit Offer**
> You can use this email as a campaign to your list or in flows such as the winback flow or abandonment flows · The psychology of having credit in your account hits differently than 10% OFF · Using an email that looks automated and uses a random number like $17.63 makes it stand out and look official · Utilizes a simple discount code... it essentially is just $17.63 OFF but framed as store credit

**2. First X Customers Sale**
> Adds urgency to get instant action from users by saying only the first X number of people can claim · A simple sale like 10% OFF for 72 hours doesn't create urgency for the user to buy NOW... they typically will delay for the last minute. This structure prevents that from happening · This store may only get 20 sales, but the customer doesn't know that. Feel free to use a random number that is super high up · This email also is a mystery discount... the offer isn't shown. Feel free to let people know it is 20% OFF or whatever the offer is

**3. Order Lottery Offer**
> 10% OFF is cool... but what if you could get your whole order for free? · This email has broken multiple client records for the highest revenue generating campaign · People will spend a lot of money knowing they could get it for free · Simply send a follow up email to everybody who bought and announce who the winners are, and give them a refund in Shopify

**4. Mystery Discount Campaign**
> Another great way to get more engagement on a sale than doing a basic % or $ OFF · People are interested in what the offer is so they will go to the site and add a product to checkout just to see the offer · Always give them the highest offer that you tease in the email so they feel special and like they have to buy

**5. The Looong Testimonial**
> Stacking 5-10 very small testimonials in an email don't do too much and customers usually skip over them · Including one very long, passionate customer review really stands out · People can connect to the story of the writer · More emotional and effective

**6. Offer Is "On Us"**
> We've found that the wording "On Us" performs better than something like "Free Shipping On Your Order" · "On Us" gives the impression that the brand is paying out of pocket for the customer so it holds more weight · Triggers reciprocity as people feel like they need to use the offer because the brand is doing them a good deed

**7. Price Reframe From Founder**
> Overcome the price objection with a note from the founder... reframing the price and doubling down on benefits · Price Reframe: "Less than your morning latte" "$3/day" · Justify / Explain Price: "We spend 50 times more on our ingredients" "Human grade standards" · Reframe Benefits... More Than Dog Food: "You're investing in more healthy years together, more energy for walks, fewer vet visits, and the peace of mind that comes with knowing you're giving them the best"

**8. Breaking News Campaign**
> For some reason these Breaking News emails crush · Stand out in the inbox by making your sale or restock or email look like a breaking news old newspaper · Adds a level of "specialness" to an offer you're running

**9. Leaked Email / Slack Message**
> Would a basic "low in stock" email perform as well as a leaked internal message about stock being low? Probably not · This is just a fun way to add urgency and get a message across · We see this perform really well for urgency campaigns

**10. Native Social Media Reviews**
> We've noticed an increase in performance when you use screenshots of testimonials from social media · They are more believable, unaltered, and personal · They stand out from the typical designed testimonial · Just go look through your social posts, ads, and DMs and screenshot any positive feedback

**11. Magic Button In Footer**
> This is a quick hack to increase clicks that works on any text-based emails · Add a "Magic Button" in your footer · People are intrigued and will click it at a very high rate. You can just direct these people to your home page, product page, or an offer page

**12. Our Stuff Isn't Cheap**
> If you have an expensive product, address it up front · Explain WHY you have high prices and explain how you don't cut corners like your competitors might · Turn your "negative" of high prices into a positive

**13. Loyalty Points Offer**
> THIS IS NOT AN ACTUAL LOYALTY PROGRAM EMAIL · This is a $ OFF offer that this brand framed as loyalty points and sent to the customer · The psychology works differently and makes the customer feel like it would be a waste to not spend the offer · Very high conversions with a simple psychology switch

**14. What's Hiding In Your X**
> If you use natural ingredients, create an email like this showing the bad ingredients that are in your competitors · Feature an ingredient list of crazy chemicals · Show how you keep things natural

**15. The "Back In Stock"**
> You don't necessarily need something to go "Out of Stock" to create a back in stock email... · If something is back in stock there is urgency built into it... that means it went out of stock · Even if you always had product, mix in back in stock campaigns to create urgency and pick up sales

**16. What's The Discount Code Campaign**
> We get REALLY high engagement on these guess the discount code campaigns · Give 4-10 different discount codes but only one of them unlocks the offer your are highlighting · People love games... so they will pick an item and go to checkout and guess what the code is. At that point they are at checkout and one button away from buying

**17. Voice Note From The Founder**
> Get an extra push by creating a custom voice note from the founder talking about the sale or any campaign really · You can embed a voice note on a page on your site OR to keep it simple you could do a simple Google Drive · This personal feel is a great way to stand out and performs really well

**18. Free Mystery Gift**
> You may notice a them here... mystery offers · This mystery gift campaign gets great engagement as people are super curious on what it is · Set this up by taking users to a landing page showing the free gift OR just do a discount code that adds the free gift

**19. The Reddit**
> People know the Reddit format and are drawn to it · Feature a Reddit testimonial or review, as it is clearly unbiased and made by someone separate from the brand · We see these perform better than standard testimonials

**20. Split Welcome Flow By Pop-Up Answer**
> We run a lot of pop-up forms that ask the customer a question such as what problem they are looking to solve with their product... · You can segment your welcome flow based on the answer that they person gave in the pop-up and give them specific information · We've noticed a meaningful impact on conversions doing this

**21. The Optimized Welcome Email**
> This is a the welcome email layout that performs the best · Notice the button and discount way up at the top of the email · Discount that looks custom for the user (often converts better than a static WELCOME10 since the customer knows that won't expire) · Educational bridge section · Product section with strong education

**22. Welcome Flow Check-In**
> Throw this email at the end of your welcome flow to get an extra push · Subject line of "Everything okay?" gets a ton of engagement from users · Reach out from support or from a real person and highlight how they haven't placed their order and ad personalization / urgency by extending the offer

**23. Abandoned Checkout Email #1**
> The headline "Your Order Is Ready to Ship" for abandoned cart / checkout email #1 absolutely rips · This is the winning headline, copy, and layout for your core abandonment email · As a bonus, include some product education

**24. Abandonment FAQ Email From Founder**
> Use this email in abandonment flows to address objections from the customer · Coming from the founder makes it more personal and hold more weight · Pick 3-5 common objections / questions then just give the answers in a friendly tone

**25. Abandonment Check-In From Founder**
> We typically use this as email 2 or 3 in abandonment flows · Reach out from the founder as a personal touch · Provide social proof and an easy way to complete the order

**26. Abandonment Mystery Discount**
> Everybody does a basic 10-20% discount on abandonments but that is boring and doesn't move the needle · Doing a "mystery discount" that reveals itself at checkout is way more intriguing + forces the customer to go to checkout again

**27. Custom Order Confirmation**
> Your order confirmation emails is one of the highest engaging emails you will ever have · Move your confirmation off of Shopify and create a custom email that is branded and has links to the site · More people will click on this than you think... just that Shop Now button in the footer will get you revenue

**28. Post-Purchase Thank You**
> This text-based email from the founder right after someone places a purchase absolutely crushes · Send this immediately after someone buys · Include a meaningful brand story, the mission, and a simple upsell

**29. Founder Check-in 3 Weeks After Purchase**
> The repeat purchases on this email are very high · Positive brand association by having the founder / VP reach out to the customer · Good reply rates and feedback for support team

# Por que a imagem importa mesmo tendo o racional

Os cartões "Notes" acima são **paráfrase, não transcrição**. Está provado: no Price Reframe From Founder, o racional escreve `"comes with knowing"` onde a peça diz `comes from knowing`, e vende uma âncora de `"$3/day"` que não aparece em lugar nenhum do e-mail (lá está `That $3 you spend today`).

Consequência: **nenhuma frase entre aspas nos racionais acima pode ser tratada como copy real** sem conferir contra a peça. As notas de `Referencias/email/` fazem essa checagem e registram cada divergência encontrada.
